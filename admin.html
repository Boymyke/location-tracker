    Things & where to find them

    Objects are places on the map (bed, fridge, wardrobe…). Items are things kept in or on them (keys, remote, charger). People can search for either and get walked there.

    Train the camera

    Teach the app what each spot looks like. Stand in a spot, press record and slowly turn around. Record each spot at least twice, at different times of day. The app then recognises where people are from their camera, fixes their position while they walk, and re-routes if they go somewhere else.

    Learn from trips

    Every trip people take is recorded: steps, compass, rooms entered, corrections, and where they left the route. This uses those trips to correct real walking distances, avoid routes people keep abandoning (something is blocking them), and spot doors that are missing from the map.

    Press “Analyse trips” to learn from everything recorded so far.
